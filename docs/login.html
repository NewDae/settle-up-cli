YOUR ACCOUNT · YOUR AGENT

One private sign-in.
Then back to your agent.

Connect your Settle Up account to the CLI that sent you here. Your agent can then access your groups, expenses, and balances, and make changes you request.

Checking your login link…

Start with your agent.

Ask your agent to run:

settleup auth login --browser

It will give you a private login link and a verification code. Open that link here. You can sign in on a different computer from the CLI.

VERIFY YOUR CONNECTION

Only continue if you started this connection yourself. The link expires in 10 minutes.

Your credentials are sent over HTTPS to this independent integration’s hosted auth service, which verifies them with Firebase. The login flow does not save your password. A temporary encrypted session is handed to your CLI.

You’re signed in.

Go back to your agent. It can finish connecting with:

settleup auth login --complete

You can close this page. To remove the CLI’s saved session later, run settleup auth logout.
