How auth works
HUMANMACHINE

Sign in to Settle Up.

Connect your account to your CLI.

Checking your login link…

Ask your agent for a login link, or run:

settleup auth login --browser

Only continue if you just asked your agent or CLI for this link. Whoever created it gets access to the account you sign in with.

Your password is checked with Firebase, Settle Up’s sign-in provider, and never saved. Your agent and CLI never see it; the CLI receives an encrypted session only it can open. How auth works ↗

You’re signed in.

Return to your agent, or finish in your CLI. It will show the connected email; check that it’s yours.

settleup auth login --complete

To disconnect later, run settleup auth logout.
